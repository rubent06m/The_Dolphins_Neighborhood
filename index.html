Home Page

Welcome to The Dolphin's Neighborhood! My very first website made with HTML and CSS. As soon as I got the thought of doing a website my first idea was to theme it around dolphins, since it's my favorite animal and I get recognized by friends pretty much by a dolphin profile or name-related. Since I was focused more on the design and esthetic of the website itself, I tried my best at capturing that Neocities vibe from the 2000s and I think I did a good job doing so. It's my first website, so there ain't too much yet going on for now, but there is more to come soon. Enjoy!

What is Neocities?

Neocities is a free commercial web hosting service that brings
back the spirit of 1990s and early 2000s Geocities era by letting
you upload your own personal website and share it with other
people by using raw HTML, CSS, Javascript and image files. The
best thing about neocities is its remarkable and unique retro
style across uncountable pages that combine the art of coding
with hobbies, blogs, art and many more. Designing a web page
and frontend in general is one of my main interests in coding so
I knew right away I would get attached to Neocities and its style.

About this Website
